# Designer fonts

Latin subsets (woff2) of fonts published under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, as distributed by
Google Fonts. They are embedded so invitations can load them from their own origin (the render CSP
allows `font-src 'self'` only). Each family's full license text is at
https://openfontlicense.org and on the family's page at fonts.google.com.

Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, Lora, Libre Baskerville, Cinzel, Italiana, Inter, Montserrat,
Josefin Sans, Poppins, Great Vibes, Parisienne, Dancing Script, Allura.
